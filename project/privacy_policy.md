# **Privacy Policy for Kone.vc ChatGPT Mini-App**

*Last updated: 01.12.2025*

## **1. Introduction**

This Privacy Policy explains how "Kone.vc" ChatGPT mini-app (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our ChatGPT-based mini-application. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## **2. Information We Collect**

### **2.1 Information You Provide**

When using the App, you may provide:

* **Text inputs**, prompts, or messages you send to the chatbot.
* **Feedback or support requests** submitted through the App.

### **2.2 Automatically Collected Information**

Depending on your platform or device, we may collect:

* **Usage data** (app interactions, feature usage, session duration).
* **Device information** (device type, OS version, browser type).
* **Log data** (IP address, timestamps, error logs).

### **2.3 Data Processed by OpenAI**

Your text inputs may be sent to **OpenAI** to generate responses.
OpenAI may temporarily store and process this data to provide the service, per their **Usage Policies** and **Privacy Practices**.

We do **not** share your data with any third parties except OpenAI for processing chatbot functionality.

---

## **3. How We Use the Information**

We may use collected information for:

* Providing and improving chatbot interactions.
* Personalizing user experience.
* Debugging, analytics, and performance optimization.
* Responding to user inquiries and support requests.
* Ensuring security and preventing misuse.

We do **not** sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
